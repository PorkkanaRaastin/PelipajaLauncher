PELIPAJA
========

Start: double-click "Pelipaja.exe". Nothing needs to be installed.
Tip: right-click the exe -> Send to -> Desktop (create shortcut).

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose "More info" and
"Run anyway" (only on your own computer).

Accounts live in the cloud: sign in with the same username/email and
password on any computer and your playtime comes with you.

Creating an account: username + email -> a 6-digit code arrives by email
(check your spam folder too) -> password.

Your account, playtime and saves are in the cloud and in the folder
%APPDATA%\pelipaja, so they stay even if you download Pelipaja again.

Updates: Pelipaja checks GitHub for a newer version every time it starts
and downloads it by itself. Updates are signed, so Pelipaja only installs
official Pelipaja versions.
