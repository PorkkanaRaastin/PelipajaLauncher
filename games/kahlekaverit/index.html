Pate and Masa are chained to each other. Empty the old mansion before morning and carry everything to the van. Every bump eats into an item's value. And don't wake Ilkka, the mansion's self-aware robot vacuum.

Big items like the piano, aquarium and marble statue need two carriers. Alone you can only drag them. Furniture in the way? Both grab it and you can move it. Every hard hit against a wall or another item lowers its value. The value is shown above the item. Shout at the same time while carrying the same item: HEAVE-HO gives you extra strength. Shouting and crashing attract Ilkka. The van is safe, Ilkka doesn't go outside. If Ilkka vacuums your friend, drag them by the chain or go next to them to wake them up.
