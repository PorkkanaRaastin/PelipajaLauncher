      Cubeland

      An endless low-poly cube world: villages, caves, ravines and lava, plains, forests, deserts and mountains. A day lasts 10 minutes and a night 10 minutes. Monsters roam at night and you keep getting hungry: build a shelter, farm, eat, trade with villagers and survive.

      The game needs a mouse and keyboard. Open it on a computer.
